## Boundaries

Desktop keyboard/mouse is the target. Mobile touch movement is not implemented. Trees are harvestable objects; wildflowers, mushrooms, and tall grass must be cleared before building. Cave background walls are non-colliding and not placeable. The gem shop has 16 offers and four functional permanent upgrades. There is no multiplayer, backend, or music track. Frame rate depends on browser/GPU; rendering uses viewport pools, capped effects, and bounded drops, with a 60 FPS target.

Browser tests use isolated local test worlds. Screenshots in `test-results/` are regenerated by the visual test and are not part of the production assets.

The market update adds regression coverage for one-tile tunnel clearance, persistent foliage clearing and placement refusal, all 16 shop rewards, exact gem deductions, duplicate equipment, insufficient funds, full-backpack bundle atomicity, old-save compatibility, equipment persistence, and real speed/mining modifiers.

The guide update verifies exact sign bounds and first-frame removal, all six onboarding tasks through real gameplay actions, completion persistence after reload, and backward-compatible guide saves. The header and guide use translucent blurred glass; narrow viewports show only the current guide task to keep gameplay visible.

Guide completion now displays an eight-second dismissible congratulations banner and plays a four-note fanfare. The completion browser test verifies a single sound trigger and no replay after reload; the audio test verifies note scheduling and mute behavior.
