# Asset provenance

## Included artwork and audio

All tiles, the explorer, white door, trees, plants, signs, inventory icons, gems, clouds, particles, and scenery were drawn specifically for Mosslight in `src/game/art/`. No external asset pack is included. Sound effects are original oscillator/envelope compositions in `src/game/systems/AudioSystem.ts`.

The interface uses locally available system fonts (Segoe UI / sans-serif, Georgia / serif, and monospace). It downloads no fonts or other media.

## Asset research

[Kenney's Pixel Platformer](https://kenney.nl/assets/pixel-platformer) and [Platformer Art Pixel](https://kenney.nl/assets/platformer-art-pixel) were checked on their official source pages, which identify them as CC0. Neither is included or adapted: original 32-pixel assets were chosen for a consistent visual identity.

## Engine

[Phaser](https://phaser.io/), version 4.2.1, by Phaser Studio and contributors, is used under its MIT license. Its copyright/license notice is retained in the distributed engine bundle. Vite, TypeScript, and testing/formatting packages retain their respective package licenses.

Growtopia is referenced only as a gameplay inspiration in the original brief. This project does not use its art, characters, logo, music, sound effects, or world designs and is not affiliated with it.
